Business Plan — AI-Powered Social Media Agency (Small Businesses)

Prepared for Ruveda

1) Executive Summary

Launch a hybrid business model:

  • Phase 1: Productized service (done-for-you + automation)
  • Phase 2: SaaS platform (self-serve automation)

Goal: help small business owners post consistently with minimal effort.

2) Problem

  • No time for content creation
  • Inconsistent posting
  • No clear content strategy
  • Traditional agencies are expensive

3) Solution

  • Monthly content ideas
  • AI-generated captions + post text
  • Branded templates
  • Scheduled publishing
  • Optional human review

4) Target Market

Small businesses (1–20 employees), especially owner-led local service businesses: salons, clinics, cafés, gyms, real estate, etc.

5) Offer Strategy (What to Sell First)

Phase 1 (Months 1–3): Instagram Growth Pack (12 posts/month, captions + hashtags, scheduling, monthly calendar)

  • Basic: €149
  • Growth: €299
  • Premium: €499

Phase 2 (Months 4–8): Semi-automated productized service (€79–€199/month).

Phase 3 (Months 9–12): SaaS launch with self-serve posting workflows.

  • Starter: €29
  • Growth: €79
  • Pro: €149

6) Revenue Model

  • Monthly subscriptions (main)
  • Optional onboarding fee (€99–€299)
  • Add-ons: reels, strategy calls, ad creatives

7) Go-To-Market

  1. Pick one niche first
  2. Offer pilot to 3–5 clients at discount
  3. Collect case studies/testimonials
  4. Use Instagram + LinkedIn + local outreach
  5. Add referral incentive

8) Operations & Automation Stack

  • AI writing: OpenAI / Claude
  • Templates: Canva
  • Automation: Make.com (or n8n)
  • Scheduling: Buffer / Metricool
  • CRM + onboarding: Notion/Airtable + Tally

Workflow: Onboarding form → AI drafts → Template fill → Approval → Scheduling → Monthly report.

9) Financial Targets (Year 1 Example)

  • Months 1–2: 3 clients × €299 = €897 MRR
  • Months 3–4: 8 clients × €299 = €2,392 MRR
  • Month 6: 15 clients × avg €320 = €4,800 MRR
  • Month 12: €8k–€15k MRR (service + early SaaS)

10) Key Metrics

  • Client retention
  • Publishing consistency
  • Engagement lift
  • Lead generation per client
  • Production time per client

11) Risks & Mitigation

  • Generic AI content → niche templates + human QA
  • Client churn → reporting + first-30-day quick wins
  • Too much manual work → SOPs + automation
  • Build SaaS too early → validate service first

12) 30-Day Action Plan

Week 1: choose niche, finalize packages, create onboarding form

Week 2: build templates and automation workflow

Week 3: onboard 3 pilot clients and deliver first calendar

Week 4: gather testimonials, improve pricing + process
